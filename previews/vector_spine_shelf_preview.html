Color Palette Harmony

Extracted the authentic 7-tone palette: Coral Pink, Royal Purple, Warm Goldenrod, Midnight Indigo, Forest Emerald, Lavender Lilac, and Wine Burgundy. Rich, vibrant, and cozy.

Vector Motifs & Frames

Faithfully recreated the vector geometry in pure SVG: Oval Cameos, Folk Crosses, Concentric Square Panels, Quatrefoils, and Calligraphic Knots.

Legible Title Integration

Unlike raster graphics, these SVG vector spines cleanly embed your book titles inside the ornate center compartments and plaques in serif and Roman fonts.
